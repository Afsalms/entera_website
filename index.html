01 · Gastro · Entero · Logia

Gastroenterology

Gastroenterology covers the whole digestive tract, from the oesophagus and stomach to the small intestine and colon. Its conditions range from common, everyday symptoms to chronic disease that shapes a patient’s life.

The field spans

  • Acid-related disorders
  • Functional gut disorders
  • Motility
  • Inflammatory bowel disease
  • Gut microbiome
02 · Hepar · Logia

Hepatology

Hepatology is the study and care of the liver, gallbladder and biliary tree. The liver performs hundreds of functions for the body, and liver disease often progresses quietly before it is found.

The field spans

  • Fatty liver disease
  • Liver protection
  • Biliary health
  • Chronic liver disease
03 · Nutrire · to nourish

Clinical Nutrition

Clinical nutrition treats nutrition as part of medical care. It supports patients through illness, surgery and recovery, and helps those whose digestive or liver conditions affect how they absorb nutrients.

The field spans

  • Malnutrition
  • Recovery & convalescence
  • Gut-health nutrition
  • Specialised nutrition
